Heritage and Craft

Civitanova Marche Workshop

Every pair is shaped in the Le Marche shoemaking district of Italy, combining three generations of welt craftsmanship with modern biomechanical lasts.

Tuscan Vacchetta

Full-grain calfskin tanned naturally with tree barks and plant extracts in Tuscany. It develops an intimate patina with time, molding uniquely to the owner’s foot.

Bio-Circular Cushioning

Outsoles cast from circular bio-polymers and natural latex. Each sole delivers cloud-soft impact absorption while ensuring full resoleability for decades of wear.
